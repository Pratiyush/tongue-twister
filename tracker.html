3 · Improvements, ranked by impact ÷ effort

StatusIdeaWhy it mattersHowEffort
Karaoke word highlightLearners follow along word by word. This is the biggest perceived-quality gain from the new voices.The ElevenLabs /with-timestamps endpoint returns character timings. Save them next to each mp3 and highlight in sync with playback.S–M
Record yourself & compare"I said it!" is an honour system. Hearing your own attempt next to the native one is the core practice loop.Use the mic (the record package) for an A/B playback screen. Keep everything on the device; nothing is uploaded.M
Speed challenge with a timerGives a reason to come back: "3× in 6.2 s, your best is 5.8 s".Time the run, store the best per twister, and show the native speaker's clip length as a target.S
Accuracy scoringObjective feedback on what you actually said.On-device speech-to-text (speech_to_text), then word-level diff against the twister. Hindi recognition quality varies, so start with English and German.M–L
Daily streak + gentle reminderDrives retention in practice apps.Count the days in a row with ≥1 attempt; add an optional local notification.S
Localise the app UIThe UI is English-only. Your other apps ship ~40 languages, and Hindi/German learners would expect at least those UI languages.flutter_localizations + ARB files; start with en/hi/de, then scale.M
More sectionsSpanish, French and Japanese twisters are popular. With ElevenLabs, a new language now costs content, not voice engineering.Follow "Adding a language" in AGENTS.md, and only add authentic twisters.M each
Smaller clips100 × ~60 KB ≈ 6 MB at 128 kbps. Speech sounds identical at 64 kbps.Switch output_format to mp3_44100_64, which roughly halves the size.XS
